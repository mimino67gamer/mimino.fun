Clean drop

How it works

Tap any block of the building to plant a charge there. Each charge has a fuse: the number of seconds after you press Detonate before it goes off. Pick a charge type and fuse before placing, or tap a placed charge to change it, drag it to move it, or remove it.

You get paid for two things. Drop is how much of the building ends up below the red target line. Containment is how much of the rubble stays between the striped drop-zone markers. Hitting a neighbouring house, car or tank counts as collateral damage and costs points and stars.

Every building is laid up out of real pieces: brick by brick, pane by pane, panel by panel. Each piece cracks from blast pressure and from hard impacts, and each material comes apart in its own way, so a well-sequenced collapse does much of the work for you. Take out the supports on one floor and let the weight above do the rest.

Keyboard: 1–4 pick a charge, +/− fuse, Del remove, Enter detonate or skip ahead, R restart, M sound.
