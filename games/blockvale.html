BLOCKVALE

A two-handed keyboard 2D block sandbox with nine biomes, flowing water and lava, villages, machinery and weather. Dig, build, craft, survive the night.
Left hand — A / D: move   Space: jump, swim, fly
S: crouch (sneak along edges, block with a shield), climb down
Z / X or 1–9: hotbar   G: drop item
E: inventory, armor and left hand (arrows + Enter)
Tab (hold): sprint   V: world map
B: waypoint   C: minimap
F: reset aim   H: hide the key list   Esc: pause
Right hand — arrow keys: move the block cursor
Right Shift (hold): mine or attack at the cursor
Enter: place, use, eat, open, ride
Right Ctrl: use the left-hand item
Every key can be rebound in Settings
Mouse is optional: it aims and clicks as well

Beyond the world: build an obsidian frame and light it with flint and steel to open a portal to the Emberdeep, a burning realm of ember brick fortresses. Deep underground lies a buried stronghold whose void frame, filled with void eyes, opens the way to the Voidlands — where crystal-shielded Void Wyrm waits.

Punch a tree for logs, make planks and sticks, then a crafting table and a wooden pickaxe. Lay items in the crafting grid like a picture, or tap a recipe in the book to fill it in. Stone and ores need a pickaxe; shovels dig dirt and sand fast, axes chop wood. Deep down the stone turns to dark deepslate. Smelt raw iron and gold in a furnace (8 cobblestone) with coal or wood. A hoe turns dirt into farmland: plant seeds from tall grass, harvest wheat, bake bread.

Six biomes stretch across a world five times wider: plains, forest, desert, snowy tundra with ice and spruces, swamp with mushrooms, and mountains with snowy peaks. Hens, pigs, cows and fish join the puffles: shear puffles for wool, milk cows with a bucket, collect eggs, and bake cookies and cake with sugar from sugar cane. Store loot in chests, climb ladders, wear armor. Watch the hunger bar next to your hearts: food keeps you healing; at zero you slowly starve. Eat apples, bread, or cooked meat. Spears can be thrown with right-click (or a tap) and picked up again.

Puffles graze the plains by day and drop meat. After dark and deep in caves, Nightlings hunt you and tall green Bloatlings swell up, hiss and explode. Craft a sword, keep torches lit, and know that nightlings burn at dawn. Nightlings drop rotten flesh (edible, if you dare); embers sometimes hide in coal ore and make lamps; bloatling powder makes TNT. Tools wear out (watch the bar under the icon), and hitting a creature while falling lands a critical hit.

New in this version: worlds save in your browser and as files; water flows and lava burns; beds skip the night; doors, levers, buttons, pressure plates, spark wire, lamps and pistons build machines; bows, arrows, skeletons and wall-climbing spiders; fishing; anvil upgrades; mineshafts, ruins, desert temples and swamp huts to loot; villages with traders who deal in emeralds; rails, minecarts and boats; a minimap and world map with waypoints; jungle, savanna and mushroom biomes plus deep dark caverns; slabs, stairs, fences, panes, dyes, paintings and signs; carrots, potatoes, melons, pumpkins and animal breeding; rain, snow and thunderstorms; achievements and stats; a Creative mode where you fly and build with everything.

Press Enter to play. Every world is generated from its seed; it autosaves. Keys can be rebound in Settings.
